About

Sascha Rust is Co-Founder and Head of Product & Impact at HarvestStack, a purpose-led private entity building responsible technology with and for the food industry. He holds a Masters of Environment and Sustainability from Monash University and a degree in International Business from University of Queensland. He is an Australian Institute of Company Directors Graduate and a Banksia Foundation award winner.

Before all of this, there were kitchens. Years of them, where he first learned the practical impact of failing systems.

Sascha maintains an AgriFood commercial advisory role with New Zealand Trade & Enterprise.
